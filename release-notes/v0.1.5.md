A reference-list check, updating and uninstalling from the Settings page, and a smaller install.

## New

- **Your reference list is checked.** Quire reads the title of each entry (APA, IEEE or MLA) and looks it up in OpenAlex and Crossref, two free catalogues of published research. The report's new **References** tab shows which were found, with a link to each, and which weren't. One not found may be mistyped or may not exist; small journals, books and reports are often in neither catalogue, so check it rather than assume. Your text is also compared with the abstracts of the works you cite.
- **Update from the Settings page.** When a new release is out, **Update to …** appears on the Settings page and in the update notice in the header. On Windows the update shows its progress in a window of its own, and Quire opens again when it is done.
- **Uninstall from the Settings page.** **Uninstall Quire** on Windows opens the uninstaller, which asks before removing anything and whether to keep your drafts. On macOS and Linux, run `quire-uninstall`.
- **Quire checks for a new release each time it starts**, instead of once a day.

## Changed

- **More of your draft is searched on the web.** The web searches now come from the body of your draft only. Before, a search could go to an entry of your reference list, which the new reference check covers better.
- **The Start menu has just Quire.** Stop Quire, Update Quire and Uninstall Quire are gone from it: updating and uninstalling are on the Settings page, and Quire still stops by itself a few minutes after you close its last tab. Quire removes the old shortcuts when it opens after this update. **Settings > Apps > Quire > Uninstall** stays.
- **A smaller install.** The packages used only to build the app (about 330) are removed once it is built, and the part that checks your drafts uses one HTTP library instead of two.
- **Pages stop refreshing while their tab is in the background**, and catch up when you come back to it.

## Fixed

- The header no longer runs wider than a narrow phone screen when an update is available.
- The report's tabs stay on one line.

## Privacy

- **The titles in your reference list are sent to OpenAlex and Crossref** to look them up; nothing else from your draft is. To turn that off, add `REFERENCE_LOOKUP=off` to `app\worker\.env` (Windows) or `app/worker/.env` in the install folder.

## Updating

Use **Update Quire** from the Start menu (or run `quire-update`), or let Quire install it the next time it starts if automatic updates are on. From this version on, updates are on the Settings page. Your drafts, reports and settings are kept, and the database is brought up to date when Quire starts. Reports you already have get a **References** tab once they are checked again.
